    How I read you

    Twelve pattern-spotters guess your next throw from what both of us have done: your favorite throw, what you do after a win or a loss, what follows your last one or two throws, whether you copy me, and whether a recent run has happened before. Three of them assume you're watching me and countering my habits.

    Each guess is tried three ways. Straight, shifted up (you throw whatever beats the obvious read), and shifted down (you throw whatever it beats). That makes 36 reads. Every one is scored every round, recent rounds count more, and I throw against the read with the best record.

    When nothing is working, I throw at random. Against a player who is truly random, no one wins more than a third of rounds over time, me included. People are rarely random, and that is the whole edge.

    The fingerprint keeps me honest. Before you choose, I hash my throw with a random 32-character key and show you the result. After you choose, I reveal the key. Changing my throw would change the hash.

    Your rounds are saved in this browser only. Forgetting wipes them and starts a fresh read.
